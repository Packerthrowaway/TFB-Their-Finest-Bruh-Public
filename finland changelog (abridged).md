## **Finland Rework Changelog (abridged)**
* Winter War changes:
    * Some starting industry moved to Karjala
    * During the Winter War factories are slowly relocated from Karjala
    * Each relocation adds stability
    * Losing Karjala removes stability
    ####
    * Finland has more stats initially in the Winter War but loses them after a while
    * Events added for losing/winning with minor debuffs/benefits
    * You get a spawn-in division for the duration of the Winter War
    ####
    * Winter War timer is 180 days (was 210)
    * Winter War decision conditions changed
    ####
    * During WW Soviets get an air debuff (mainly to CAS strength and logi strike damage)
	* Claims in Baltic now requires Germany being at war (was at war with Poland), completion time reduced
    * Baltic Security focus now has to be started between November and January or after August 40
    * Added new Winter War related focuses to Soviet tree, changed position of Molotov Line focus
    ####
* Finnish focus tree changes
    * Tension requirements for some focuses lowered
    * Mannerheim Line focus now adds Fortresses, Mannerheim state modifier and Fortresses removed after WW, can be restored with new decision
    * Foreign Aircraft focus replaced with Purchase Blenheims focus
	* Modernize Production Lines focus gives production modifiers
    ####
    * Jaeger Movement focus Org modifier halved (was 5%), Breakthrough modifier removed (was 5%), SF cap increased to 7.5% (was 5%) and completion time halved (was 70 days)
    * Long Range Patrol focus unlocks new land raid against soviets
    * Motti Tactics focus now gives Mastery, no longer provides 2.5% Attacking Division Speed
    * Sissi focus now provides 3% Attacking Division Speed
    * National Armor Focus focus now spawns Land Warfare Faciltiy
    ####
    * Lone Wolf doesnt restrict faction joining, doesnt get removed anymore, and has had its modifiers changed
    * March of Conquest modifier lasts longer, doesnt give recovery rate anymore
    * Advanced Jaeger Training Program focus SF cap reduced to 2.5% (was 5%)
    * Mustapaidat focus gives Militia tech
	* Keepers Of The North focus no longer annexes AI Denmark, now unlocks decision to take Finnmark from RK Norway
    ####
    * Political tree mastery bonuses nerfed
    * Both political trees should take 35 days longer to complete and grant 5% less stability
    * Some flat Conscription modifiers increased, but Conscription Factor modifiers lowered
    ####
* Map changes
    * Åland is now a Strait blocking the Gulf of Bothnia
    * Port added to Tornio
    * Impassable borders changed
    * Added river in Karjala
    * Petrozavodsk and Kirkenes terrain changed
    * Karjala starts with modifier debuffing Bunkers
	* Häme has coal now
    ####
* Other Finland changes
	* Finland has 1 more starting mil
    * Divisions start with more equipment
    * Unique propaganda campaigns added
	* BT-42 Chassis added to Finland (BT-7 with light casemate)
    * Now uses mostly unique equipment graphics and names
    * Sisu spirit factory bombing reduction reduced
	* Unit Leaders Mannerheim and Aarne Juutilainen buffed slightly
	* Added Unique Spy (Traits: Infiltrator, Well Groomed)
